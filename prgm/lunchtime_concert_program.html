Lunchtime Concert Program

VOICES IN FLIGHT

Soprano: Xingtian Ge | Pianist: Yizhe Lin
A curated one-hour recital for soprano and piano

INTRODUCTION

Why It Works for Lunchtime

This program is ideally suited for a lunchtime recital. With a total duration of approximately one hour, it offers a high-density gala-style selection of works that fits the midday format beautifully, providing a full artistic journey within a concise frame.

The theme Voices in Flight emphasizes lightness, freedom, and soaring brilliance. The agile runs and crystalline coloratura writing create an uplifting energy that feels especially effective for a midday audience seeking a brief but inspiring spiritual escape from the routines of the day.

The duo format of voice and piano is also especially well matched to an intimate church setting, helping foster a close connection between performers and listeners, which is one of the great strengths of a successful lunchtime concert series.

Audience Experience

This concert offers the audience a multi-sensory journey of ascent. Listeners encounter the awe of virtuosity through the soprano's shimmering high tones and airborne runs, experience a wide emotional arc from Mozart's radiant joy to Schubert's yearning and Donizetti's theatrical flair, and hear a thoughtful bridge between East and West through the inclusion of Jasmine Flower.

The result is a luminous one-hour immersion that leaves the audience with a sense of lightness, freshness, and renewed energy for the rest of the day.

PROGRAMME

  • Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart Exsultate, jubilate, K. 165
  • Franz Schubert Lachen und Weinen; Suleika II
  • Richard Strauss Amor
  • Frédéric Chopin Nocturne in C minor, Op. 48, No. 1 (Piano Solo)
  • Chinese Folk Song Jasmine Flower
  • Gaetano Donizetti "Ah! tardai troppo... O luce di quest'anima" from Linda di Chamounix
  • Eva Dell'Acqua Villanelle
