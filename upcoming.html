July 02, 2026

Songs of Love: A Journey of the Heart

St. Paul's Church, Birmingham, UK

Introduction
  • Soprano: Xingtian Ge
  • Pianist: Yizhe Lin

Music has always been the most profound language of the soul, transcending the boundaries of time, geography, and culture to articulate the inexhaustible complexities of human emotion. Tonight’s program, Songs of Love, is an invitation to traverse the vast landscape of affection—a journey that begins in the celestial and anchors itself in the terrestrial. From the sacred adoration of the Divine to the tender whispers of romantic intimacy, and from the deep-seated love for one's homeland to the reverent embrace of nature—we celebrate love in all its infinite grace. We invite you to join us on this heart-centered pilgrimage, where every note serves as a tribute to the forces that unite and sustain us.

May 01, 2026

Bournville Lunchtime Concert Series

Birmingham, UK

Introduction
  • Voices in Flight - Soprano: Xingtian Ge, Pianist: Yizhe Lin

Voices in Flight is a journey into the air, carried by the human voice. In the world of vocal music, the coloratura soprano is the voice closest to flight. This sound shimmers and soars beyond the limits of range, unfolding brilliant cascades of notes high above. The agile runs, the crystalline high tones—these are not merely feats of virtuosity, but expressions of emotional freedom. Join in this concert to enjoy how the coloratura soprano writes poetry in flight.

“Orpheus in the Underworld"--Jacques OffenBach

Birmingham, UK

Introduction
  • Director: Rebecca Meltzer
  • Conductor: Paul Wingfield
  • Set/Costume Designer: Jennifer Gregory
  • Repetiteur: Yizhe Lin

Orpheus in the Underworld is surely one of Offenbach's most irreverent operas. It takes a sniper's approach to the Parisian elite, casting them as the Gods of Olympus and lampooning them for their hypocrisy and immorality. It does not take too great a leap of the imagination to map this cut-throat satire onto our own turbulent times.
